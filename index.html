The problem

Every tool sends its own bill, its own login, and its own version of the truth.

The CRM knows the client. The project tool knows the schedule. The inbox knows what was promised. Nothing knows all three, so the business runs on whoever remembers. ReCaptured puts the record in the middle and connects the rest to it.

How it works

Four things happen to everything your business touches.

A web form, a meeting, a photo from the truck, an email, a file: each one becomes part of a record, stays linked to its source, and becomes something your assistant can answer about.

1

Capture

Web forms, email, Slack, meetings and dictated notes come in through one front door. Transcribed, summarized and consent-logged. No retyping.

The note you dictate in the truck, the decision in the hallway, the follow-up after the call: work that never used to get written down becomes part of the record.

2

Connect

Everything lands on the right person, company, deal or project. Every note, file and decision keeps a link back to where it came from.

3

Organize

Built-in agents triage intake, draft tasks from transcripts, watch deadlines and SLAs, and propose who should do what next. A person approves each one.

4

Questions a careful operator asks

Straight answers.

What is ReCaptured, in one sentence?

A unified business management platform: CRM, projects, files and communications on one connected record, with an AI assistant that answers from that record. It isn't a CRM with AI bolted on, and it isn't a chatbot with a database behind it.

How do you price it?

One price per full seat: $69 a month, with a 3-seat minimum, billed monthly. Guests view for free. Your AI assistant's usage allowance is pooled across your team, and the exact allotment is stated in writing before you pay. Setup is free. Join Founding 100 now and your seat price is locked for 24 months. Full pricing →

What's the difference between the pilot and the Founding 100?

They're separate offers. The 30-day pilot is for businesses in industries we're actively building for; it lets you try ReCaptured before committing, and you apply for it. Founding 100 rewards early paying customers with a 24-month price lock. A pilot that converts while Founding 100 is open gets the lock too.

What does the AI assistant actually do today?

It answers questions from the records you can see, drafts tasks and follow-ups from meeting transcripts with the source minute attached, triages new intake with its reasoning shown, watches deadlines and SLAs and escalates before anything is forfeited, and proposes workload rebalances. Anything leaving your organization waits for a person to confirm. The full list, with what's next →

What does the assistant do without asking me?

Nothing that changes your records or leaves your organization. It drafts, suggests and surfaces, then waits. Every action it proposes shows what it will do, with which arguments, and who can approve it.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. We don't use your records to train AI models. Your assistant works only from your organization's records, and anything marked Private or Off-the-Record is never indexed. Questions about how your data is used? Email privacy@recaptured.io and you'll get a straight answer in writing.

Can we keep using Slack, Gmail, Google Drive and QuickBooks?

Yes. Slack, Gmail, Microsoft 365, Google Calendar and Google Drive connect today; threads, events and files link to records without moving. QuickBooks and Xero sync is designed and coming after launch.

Can clients, vendors and board members work in it without buying seats?

Yes. Invite them as free external guests. They see only the project or records you share, view-only, and when they join a recorded meeting they are asked for consent like every other participant, and it is time-stamped.

Can we build our own automations?

Today you get the built-in agents: triage, drafting from transcripts, deadline and SLA guardian, enrichment and rebalancing, each with approval gates you control. A builder for your own plain-English automations is designed and coming after launch; we'll say so on every page rather than promise a date.

What happens if we leave?

Export your records, files and history in standard formats whenever you ask. If you leave, you can export everything for 30 days, and we delete what's left within 90 days after that, keeping only what the law requires us to keep: signed-document audit trails, security logs and invoices. Same terms for pilots and paid accounts.
