About

Small teams deserve infrastructure, not another app.

ReCaptured is one connected system for running a business of 3 to 25 people. Leads, deals, projects, people, files, meetings and chat live on a single record, and an AI assistant works from that record and nothing else.

Why we built it

We spent years watching the same problems get solved one tool at a time and never as one system. The CRM didn't know about the job. The job didn't know about the call. And the decision made in the hallway never got written down at all. Small teams felt it most, because the same three people were the ones flipping between seven apps to stitch it back together.

So we built the thing we wanted: run everything from one screen instead of flipping between apps.

Who builds it

ReCaptured is built by operators who've run field crews, sales teams and member organizations, and engineered by a CTO who has been shipping production software for three decades. We're small on purpose. When you talk to us, you're talking to the people who build it.

How we work

  • Straight answers. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you on the first call.
  • Your data stays yours. Export any time. We log our own access.
  • Plain pricing. $69 per seat, 3-seat minimum, month to month. Anything else is stated in writing before you pay.
  • Built with pilot partners. New industries start as 30-day pilots, and we don't name a partner without written approval.

What we believe

Take care of your people, add real value first, and the rest follows.

Where the assistant came from

The assistant started with one rule: notice what's missing before anyone has to ask. The follow-up nobody sent. The decision nobody wrote down. That's still its job.
