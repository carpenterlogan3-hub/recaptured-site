Pilot Terms

What a 30-day pilot includes, what we ask of you, and what happens to your data.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Offered by RECAPTURED, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("ReCaptured," "we," "us"). These terms cover 30-day pilots. Your written pilot agreement controls if it says something different.

Length

30 days unless your pilot agreement says otherwise. You can extend once by agreement.

What you get

The live product on your own data, setup help for one workflow, and a direct line to the people building it. No usage charges during the pilot.

What we ask

Real use, day to day. A named decision-maker. A short check-in each week, and an honest decision at day 30: convert, extend or stop, with reasons we can learn from.

Your data

It's yours. If you stop, you can export it for 30 days, and we delete what's left within 90 days after that.

Publicity

We never name a pilot partner publicly without written approval.

Converting

If you convert to a paid subscription while Founding 100 is open, you qualify for the price lock. See the Founding 100 Terms.

Law

Delaware law governs these terms.
