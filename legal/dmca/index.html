Copyright and DMCA Policy

How to send a copyright notice or counter-notice to our designated agent, and what we do with repeat infringers.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

RECAPTURED, LLC respects copyright and expects its users to do the same. We respond to notices of claimed infringement under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. §512.

Designated agent

Copyright infringement notices (DMCA): Send notices under 17 U.S.C. §512(c) to our Designated Agent:
DMCA Agent, Legal Department, RECAPTURED, LLC, 2305 Pennsylvania Avenue, Wilmington, DE 19806, 302-468-0457, dmca@recaptured.io

Sending a notice

Your notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. The copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  3. The material you claim is infringing and enough information for us to find it.
  4. Your contact information.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorized by the owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you're the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the agent above that includes the elements required by 17 U.S.C. §512(g)(3). We'll forward it to the original complainant and may restore the material in 10 to 14 business days unless they tell us they've filed a court action.

Repeat infringers

We terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers.

Misrepresentations

Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can create liability under 17 U.S.C. §512(f).
