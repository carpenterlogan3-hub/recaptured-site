Get started
30-day pilot

Exploring ReCaptured for your industry? Run a 30-day pilot with us.

We're partnering with a small number of businesses in industries we're actively building for. For 30 days, we set ReCaptured up around one part of your operation, train your team, and check in weekly. At day 30 you decide.

Already in an industry we serve today? You don't need a pilot: get started at Founding pricing or book a call.

How it works

  • 1
    ApplyFive minutes. Tell us the one workflow you'd want running first.
  • 2
    Fit call, 20 minutesWe confirm the workflow, the team and the success criteria, and we tell you plainly if it's not a fit yet.
  • 3
    Setup, week oneWe configure that workflow with you, connect your inbox, calendar and Drive, and train your team. No usage charges during the pilot.
  • 4
    Weekly 15-minute check-inWhat's working, what isn't, what we're changing.
  • 5
    Day-30 reviewContinue on a paid plan (the Founding 100 price lock applies if it's still open), extend once, or stop.

What we ask of you

  • ·
    Real use, day to dayA pilot on the side of your real work tells neither of us anything.
  • ·
    A named decision-makerSomeone who can say yes or no at day 30.
  • ·
    A good-faith decisionConvert, extend or stop, with reasons we can learn from.

If you stop, you can export your data for 30 days and we delete what's left within 90 days after that. We never name a pilot partner publicly without written approval. Pilot terms →
