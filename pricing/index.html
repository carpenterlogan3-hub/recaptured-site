How assistant usage works

Your AI assistant runs on a usage allowance that's pooled across your team, so one heavy user doesn't run out while everyone else has room to spare. Everyday use is included; the allowance is drawn by the heavier work it does for you. No surprise bills. You decide what happens at 100%.

Always includedAsking questions, search, indexing your records and files, and the context that follows you around the app.
Draws on the allowanceTranscription and summaries, drafted documents and replies, agent runs, bulk actions. Each call shows what it used.
You'll know before it runs outAlerts at 50%, 80% and 100% of the pool. Admins see the balance against the cycle on the dashboard.
Overage is off unless you turn it onSet a monthly ceiling, or turn on auto-refill. If you turn overage on, the rate is stated in writing before you pay.

Pricing questions

What counts as a seat?

A member of your organization who signs in and works in ReCaptured. Clients, vendors, board members and subcontractors you invite as external guests are free, see only what you share, and can only view.

What happens if we use up the allowance?

The assistant tells you at 50%, 80% and 100%. At 100%, usage-based features pause until the next cycle unless an admin has turned overage on and set a ceiling, or turned on auto-refill. Nothing is billed that you didn't choose.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time from your account settings; no call required. Monthly billing, no exit fee. Export your data before or after; it's yours.

What does Founding 100 lock, exactly?

The per-seat subscription price for full seats, for 24 months from your start date, as long as your subscription stays active. It doesn't lock usage allowances, add-ons, new products we launch later, services or taxes. Full terms →

Is there a minimum team size?

Three seats. ReCaptured is built for teams of 3–25. If you're a team of one or two, book a call anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth it yet. Bigger than 25? Let's talk.
